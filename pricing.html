Pricing FAQ.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes. The trial is free for 7 days, so if you change your mind, just cancel before day 8 and you're never charged. Annual plans get a pro-rated refund for unused time.

How do I cancel?

One click in your Whop dashboard — no retention emails, no "are you sure" loop, no phone call. You can re-subscribe later at the same rate if you're on the annual plan.

Are the picks the same on every plan?

Yes. The free trial unlocks the same premium card the monthly and annual members see. Annual subscribers additionally get strategy breakdowns and the full historical pick database.

What payment methods are accepted?

All major cards via Whop checkout. Whop is the merchant of record — we never see your card details. See our Privacy Policy for more.
