2.What "Track Record" Actually Means

Our public log shows every pick we've made since launch and every result. We post the losses too — they're a real part of any honest record. Past performance is not a predictor of future performance. A long winning streak is followed by losing days. A profitable month is followed by a losing month. That's variance, and it's normal.

If you see a sports betting service claiming 70%+ win rates over hundreds of plays, they are either lying or showing you a cherry-picked subset. Realistic long-term edge in major-market sports betting is in the 53–58% range.
