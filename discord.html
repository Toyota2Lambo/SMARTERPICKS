House rules

Keep it useful.

01
No tout posts.

Don't promote other capper services, paid groups, or affiliate links. Discuss your own bets all you want — but no recruiting.

02
Numbers, not vibes.

If you're recommending a play, post the line, the book, and your reasoning. "I just feel like this is a lock" is not analysis.

03
Receipts welcome, leaks aren't.

Posting wins and losses in #brag-board is encouraged. Reposting the premium daily card outside the server gets you removed.

04
Bet responsibly.

If gambling stops being fun, talk to someone or use 1-800-GAMBLER. We'll never push you to bet more than you're comfortable with.
